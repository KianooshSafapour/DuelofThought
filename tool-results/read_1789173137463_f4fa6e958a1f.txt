     1→     1→# Worklog — "Duel of Ideas" (دوئل ایده‌ها) Gaming Platform
     2→     2→
     3→     3→## Project Vision
     4→     4→A Persian-first (fa/en/ar/tr) PWA gaming park. Flagship game: **Duel of Ideas** — a 9×7 (63-cell) mathematical strategy board game by Iranian mathematicians. Players drop pieces into columns (gravity like Connect-4) and win by owning **4 of a kind** (e.g., four 5s) or a **run of 4 consecutive numbers** (e.g., 2,3,4,5). Numberless cells are strategic blockers. Platform includes: PvAI, local PvP, online multiplayer (socket.io), ELO leaderboard, coins/XP/levels, achievements, daily quests, in-game store (skins/avatars/backgrounds/titles), admin dashboard (analytics, bans, reports, moderation), PWA support.
     5→     5→
     6→     6→## Research applied
     7→     7→- Martinez et al. 2023: board games → fluid intelligence, working memory, social skills
     8→     8→- Trends in Neuroscience & Education 2021: board games → executive function
     9→     9→- SDT (Deci/Ryan): autonomy (customization/store), competence (levels/achievements), relatedness (multiplayer/chat)
    10→    10→- Flow (Csikszentmihalyi): AI difficulty tiers + rated matchmaking to balance challenge/skill
